Coding agents on your Android phone. Ten agents, including Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Qwen Code, Kilo, Cline, Goose and OpenCode, in a Linux the app installs itself. No computer, no Termux. Or connect to the agent on your computer.


Why
Coding agents keep working while you are away from your desk, and they stop to ask. A phone is where that question reaches you.
Setting up a Linux and an agent by hand on a phone takes a lot of steps. The app does them for you and keeps the agent inside the app.


Get started
You need an Android phone running Android 8 or newer. Running agents on the phone itself needs a 64-bit phone.


Privacy
The app connects only to the servers you choose. Passwords stay in Android's keystore, voice is transcribed on the device, and diagnostics leave the phone only when you send a report yourself, with secrets removed.
